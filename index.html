UNFPA adolescent and youth mapping: combined survey dashboard

This dashboard provides a quick, shareable glimpse of the current draft evidence from the organization profile survey, intervention-level 5Ws survey, and donor consultation survey. It uses cleaned datasets prepared as of 23 August 2026 and shows aggregate results only.

Important: figures are self-reported and operational. Reported reach is not deduplicated and may mix unique participants, cumulative participants and service contacts.

Draft data quality flags

Aggregate revision flags across cleaned datasets.

Coverage strength: the mapping has a strong intervention base for Gaza and multi-sector AY programming, especially MHPSS, protection, education, child protection and GBV.
Donor signal: donors identify SRH, GBV, TVET/employability, MHPSS, child protection, digital inclusion and livelihoods as underfunded or insufficiently scaled.
Use carefully: lower counts in West Bank and East Jerusalem should be triangulated before being interpreted as confirmed absence of services.
